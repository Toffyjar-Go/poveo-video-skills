# Example: a 60-second explainer for a billing app

*A made-up business, planned with the video planner.*

## Brief

- **Audience**: owners of small service businesses (clinics, salons, studios) who send invoices themselves.
- **Problem**: "I do the work, then lose an evening every week chasing payments."
- **One message**: the app sends the invoice and the reminder for you, the moment the job is done.
- **Proof**: a demo of one invoice going out in three taps.
- **Action**: start a free trial on the website.
- **Format**: website and YouTube, 16:9, 60 seconds.

## Hook

**Chosen (the moment):** "It's eleven at night, and the invoice still isn't sent." On screen: a laptop at night with an unfinished invoice.

Others:
- *The result first*: "This invoice took three taps."
- *Talk to one person*: "If you run a small clinic, this is for you."

## Script

| # | Seconds | Voice | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 | It's eleven at night, and the invoice still isn't sent. | 11:04 pm |
| 2 | 7 | You finished the work hours ago. It's the paperwork that keeps you up, every single week. | The work: done. The invoice: not. |
| 3 | 7 | Meet Tally: invoices that go out the moment the job is done, without the late nights. | Tally |
| 4 | 8 | Mark the appointment as finished, check the amount, and tap send. That's really all there is to it. | 1. Done 2. Check 3. Send |
| 5 | 8 | Your client gets a clear invoice with a pay button, by email and on WhatsApp, straight away. | Pay in one tap |
| 6 | 8 | If it isn't paid on time, Tally sends a polite reminder for you, so you don't have to ask. | Reminders, sent for you |
| 7 | 8 | And you can see who has paid and who hasn't, all on one simple screen. | Paid / waiting |
| 8 | 8.5 | Get your evenings back. Start your free trial today at tally-example.com. | Free trial |
| End | 1.5 | — | Logo and web address |

Spoken words: 122 (inside 120–150). Scenes add up to 60 seconds, with 1.5 for the end card.

## Shot list

1. A laptop on a kitchen table at night; a half-filled invoice.
2. Text on the brand colour; the two lines appear one after the other.
3. The logo forming; the app's home screen.
4. Screen recording: the appointment marked done, the amount, the send button tapped.
5. A phone showing the invoice arriving by WhatsApp; a thumb on Pay.
6. Simple animation: a reminder card sliding in a week later.
7. The dashboard, paid in green and waiting in amber.
8. The owner closing the laptop, early evening light; the web address.

## Brand voice

Calm, plain, on your side. We say "so you don't have to"; we don't say "automate your accounts receivable workflow". Never say "never chase a payment again" (it is not guaranteed).

## Before you make it

- Redraw the app screens so no real client names or amounts show.
- Check the trial is really free and needs no card before saying so.
