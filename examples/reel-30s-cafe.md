# Example: a 30-second Reel for a café

*A made-up business, planned with the video planner.*

## Brief

- **Audience**: students and people working near the café, ordering between classes or meetings.
- **Problem**: "I want something better than a vending-machine coffee, but I only have ten minutes."
- **One message**: our new oat latte is ready in under three minutes, made to order.
- **Proof**: the barista times one on camera.
- **Action**: order ahead on the café's WhatsApp number.
- **Format**: Instagram Reel, 9:16, 30 seconds.

## Hook

**Chosen (the number):** "Three minutes. Real coffee. No vending machine." On screen: a stopwatch starting next to the espresso machine.

Others:
- *The moment*: "Ten minutes between classes?" (a student checking the time)
- *The before and after*: "Vending machine. Or this." (a split screen)

## Script

| # | Seconds | Voice | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 | Three minutes. Real coffee. No vending machine. | 3:00 |
| 2 | 5 | Meet our new oat latte, made fresh the moment you order it. | New: oat latte |
| 3 | 6 | A fresh espresso shot, oat milk steamed smooth, and a little vanilla if you like. | Fresh. Smooth. |
| 4 | 5 | Watch the clock. Done in two minutes and forty seconds. | 2:40 |
| 5 | 5 | Perfect between classes or meetings, right here on campus. | Right on campus |
| 6 | 4.5 | Order ahead on WhatsApp, walk in, and skip the line. | Order on WhatsApp |
| End | 1.5 | — | Logo and number |

Spoken words: 63 (inside 60–75). Scenes add up to 30 seconds, with 1.5 for the end card.

## Shot list

1. Close-up of the stopwatch starting beside the machine.
2. The latte being poured, from above.
3. Espresso shot, then milk steaming; slow and close.
4. The stopwatch stopping at 2:40, the cup slid across the counter.
5. A customer with a backpack taking the cup at the door.
6. A phone sending a WhatsApp order; the café's number big on screen.

## Brand voice

Warm, quick, local. We say "made when you order it"; we don't say "artisanal handcrafted beverage". Never say "best coffee in town".

## Before you make it

- Film the real timing; if it is over three minutes, change the hook to the real number.
- The WhatsApp number must be on the end card for at least 1.5 seconds.
