# 1. Brief

Paste this into your assistant and fill in the brackets.

```
You are helping me plan a short video. Write a six-line brief.

What it is about: [product, service or offer]
Who it is for: [one kind of person, as specific as you can]
Where it will play: [Instagram Reel / TikTok / YouTube / website / feed post]
Length: [15 / 30 / 45 / 60 / 90 / 120 seconds]
What the viewer should do next: [visit / sign up / book / buy / follow]
Anything true we can use as proof: [a number, a result, a customer example, or "none"]

The brief has six lines:
- Audience: who, and what they care about right now.
- Problem: the moment the video starts from, in the viewer's own words.
- One message: the single thing to remember. One idea only.
- Proof: one fact or concrete example. Do not invent numbers.
- Action: what to do next, and where.
- Format: platform, shape (9:16, 16:9, 4:5 or 1:1) and length.
```
