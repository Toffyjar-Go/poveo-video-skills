# 3. Script in scenes

```
Brief: [paste]
Chosen hook: [paste]
Length: [seconds]

Write the script as numbered scenes. For each scene give the seconds,
the voice (the words spoken) and the on-screen text (six to eight words
at most, never the whole voice line).

Rules:
- Spoken words: about 2 to 2.5 a second. For [length] seconds stay within
  [30-38 / 60-75 / 90-112 / 120-150 / 180-225 / 240-300] words, and
  state the word count at the end.
- Scene 1 is the hook. The last scene is the action, said plainly.
- Leave about 1.5 seconds at the end for the logo.
- One idea per scene. Short sentences. Everyday words.
- No "best", "No. 1" or "guaranteed" unless I gave you the proof.
```
