# 6. Before you make it

```
Here is my video plan:
[paste brief, script, shot list and voice guide]

Check it and list only what fails, each with the fix:
- One message, said in the viewer's words; the hook is paid off; the
  action is said and shown in the last scene.
- Spoken words fit the length; scene seconds add up with time for the
  end card; on-screen text is short enough to read twice.
- Every number and claim has a source I gave; no "best", "No. 1" or
  "guaranteed" without proof; nothing from the never-say list.
- Music, footage, fonts, logos and people are ones we may use.
- The shape fits the platform; it works with the sound off; the first
  frame is not a logo.
- Names are spelt the brand's way; the end card says where to go next.
```
