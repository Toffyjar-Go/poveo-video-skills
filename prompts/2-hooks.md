# 2. Hooks

```
Here is my video brief:
[paste the brief]

Write three openings for the first two seconds, each a different kind:
the moment, the question, the result first, the surprise, the number,
the mistake, the before and after, or talking to one person.

For each: the first line (eight words or fewer), what is on screen in the
first frame (never a logo), and one line on why it works for this audience.
Then recommend one. Avoid "Hey guys", "In this video", "Did you know"
and "Are you tired of".
```
