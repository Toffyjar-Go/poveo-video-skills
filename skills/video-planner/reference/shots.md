# Shots

Describe each scene so someone who has never seen the product can find or make it.

| Shot | Use it for | Describe |
|---|---|---|
| Product close-up | Physical products, food, packaging | The object, the angle, the light |
| Screen recording | Apps and websites | Which screen, what is tapped, what changes |
| Redrawn screen | App screens that must stay sharp or hide data | The screen simplified to its key parts |
| Person to camera | Trust, founders, testimonials | Who, where, framing (head and shoulders) |
| Hands at work | Services and how-tos | The task, close and from above |
| Text on colour | Numbers, quotes, the one message | The words, big; the brand colour |
| Stock clip | Settings you cannot film | The subject and mood, not a brand |
| Simple animation | Ideas, flows, before and after | What moves and in what order |

Rules:

- One subject per shot. If you need "and", it is two shots.
- Show the brand where it belongs (on the product, the screen, the end card), not as a watermark in every shot.
- Prefer real material the business already has over stock.
- Vertical (9:16): keep the subject in the middle, with the top and bottom eighth free for the app's buttons and captions.
