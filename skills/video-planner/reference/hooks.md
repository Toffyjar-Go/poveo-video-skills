# Hooks: the first two seconds

Most people decide in the first two seconds whether to keep watching. A hook earns the third second. Pick three different kinds for each video.

| Kind | How it works | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **The moment** | Start in the exact situation the viewer is in | "It's 11 pm and the invoice still isn't sent." |
| **The question** | Ask the question they are already asking themselves | "Still copying orders into a spreadsheet?" |
| **The result first** | Show the end result, then how you got there | "This took four minutes. Here's how." |
| **The surprise** | Say something that sounds wrong, then explain it | "Your slowest step isn't the work. It's the waiting." |
| **The number** | A specific, true number | "Three taps. That's the whole checkout." |
| **The mistake** | Name a common mistake | "Most cafés price their latte wrong." |
| **The before and after** | Two states, side by side | "Before: six tabs. After: one." |
| **Talk to one person** | Name who it is for | "If you run a small clinic, this is for you." |

Rules:

- Say it in under two seconds: eight words or fewer.
- Show something in the first frame that matches the words; never a logo first.
- The hook must be true to the rest of the video. A hook the video does not pay off loses trust.
- Avoid "Hey guys", "In this video", "Did you know" and "Are you tired of".
