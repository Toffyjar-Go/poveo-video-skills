# Before you make it

Go through each line. Report only what fails, with the fix.

**Message**
- [ ] One message, and the video says it in words a viewer would use.
- [ ] The hook is paid off by the rest of the video.
- [ ] The action is said plainly in the last scene, and on screen.

**Length and timing**
- [ ] Spoken words are inside the range for the length.
- [ ] Scene seconds add up to the length, with time for the end card.
- [ ] Each scene has one idea and on-screen text short enough to read twice.

**Truth and safety**
- [ ] Every number and claim has a source the person gave you.
- [ ] No "best", "No. 1" or "guaranteed" without proof.
- [ ] Nothing from the never-say list.
- [ ] Music, footage, fonts and logos are ones they own or are allowed to use.
- [ ] People shown have agreed to be in it.

**Platform**
- [ ] The shape matches where it plays (9:16, 16:9, 4:5, 1:1).
- [ ] It makes sense with the sound off (captions or on-screen text).
- [ ] The first frame is not a logo or a blank screen.

**Brand**
- [ ] Names spelt the way the brand writes them.
- [ ] The voice matches the guide.
- [ ] The end card has the brand, and where to go next.
