# Brand voice

A voice guide fits on half a page.

1. **Three adjectives**, each with a pair:
   - *Warm*: we say "Come in, the coffee's on." We don't say "Valued customers are welcome."
   - *Plain*: we say "Send the invoice in one tap." We don't say "Leverage seamless invoicing."
   - *Confident*: we say "It works on day one." We don't say "We hope it might help."
2. **Words we use**: the customer's own words for the product and the problem.
3. **Never say**: words that are off-brand, risky or overused (for example "revolutionary", "game-changer", "cheap", "guaranteed"), and anything legally sensitive in the industry (health claims, returns on money).
4. **Spelling and names**: how the brand and product names are written, and the country's spelling.

Check every line of the script against it before you finish.
