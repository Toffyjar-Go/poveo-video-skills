# Timing

Natural voiceover runs at about 2 to 2.5 words a second. Leave about 1.5 seconds at the end for the logo or end card.

| Video length | Spoken words | Scenes (Reel) | Scenes (explainer) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 seconds | 30–38 | 3–4 | — |
| 30 seconds | 60–75 | 5–8 | 4–5 |
| 45 seconds | 90–112 | 7–10 | 6–7 |
| 60 seconds | 120–150 | — | 8–10 |
| 90 seconds | 180–225 | — | 11–14 |
| 120 seconds | 240–300 | — | 14–18 |

- Fast styles (bold text on screen, quick cuts) sit at the top of each range; calm styles at the bottom.
- If the script is over, cut words. Never ask for a faster voice.
- On-screen text is read in about a second per three to four words; keep it short enough to read twice.
